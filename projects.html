No. 010.66ROC AUC on a balanced test set

Customer Churn Prediction

Data Science & Statistics

Random Forest model that ranks telecom customers by churn risk and puts a revenue figure on the customers lost.

Problem

Which telecom customers are likely to leave, and what does losing them cost per month and per year?

Approach

Exploratory analysis (class balance, cost of churn, correlations), one-hot encoding, median imputation, a stratified train-test split and a Random Forest classifier.

Result

AUC of 0.66, enough to segment customers by risk. Evaluated with a confusion matrix, accuracy and ROC curve, and read in business terms: the cost of a missed churner against a false alarm.

No. 024classifiers compared, chosen on recall

CDR Analytics & Anomaly Detection

Data Science & Statistics

Predicts whether a call will be picked up and flags unusual calls with a custom suspicion score.

Problem

In a call detail record dataset, which calls get answered, and which calls look out of place?

Approach

Time features engineered from raw timestamps. Logistic Regression, Logistic GAM, Random Forest and Naive Bayes compared. A suspicion score built from department-pair rarity and standardised call duration.

Result

Selected the model with the fewest false negatives while keeping precision and accuracy strong. Flagged the five most anomalous calls and confirmed them against departmental traffic patterns.

No. 033/3WLS models beat the OLS benchmark

HAR-GARCH Volatility Forecasting

Data Science & Statistics · Honours project, 2025

Honours research: weighting HAR models with GARCH volatility estimates improved S&P 500 volatility forecasts out of sample.

Problem

OLS residuals in the standard HAR model showed heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation, which weakens estimation and inference.

Approach

Weighted Least Squares with conditional volatility as weights, comparing sGARCH, GJR-GARCH and AVGARCH weighting schemes in R (rugarch).

Result

Every WLS model outperformed the OLS benchmark out of sample on MSE and QLIKE loss. The GJR-GARCH-weighted model performed best.

Software Engineering · Live, in active development

Problem
